About Us

Who We Are

Balance & Byte was created to help small businesses manage their websites and accounting in a simple, structured way.

We noticed that many SMEs struggle with the same challenges:

  • Websites that become outdated or difficult to maintain
  • Accounting tasks that pile up and feel overwhelming
  • Multiple vendors to manage for different services

Our goal is to provide reliable monthly services that remove these burdens and give business owners peace of mind.

What We Do

We offer subscription-based website management and accounting services designed specifically for small and growing businesses.

Instead of one-off projects or unpredictable costs, our approach focuses on:

  • Predictable monthly pricing
  • Ongoing support and maintenance
  • Long-term partnership

We believe businesses should not have to worry about technical issues or falling behind on their financial records.
